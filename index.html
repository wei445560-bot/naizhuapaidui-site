关于我们 About

南京奶爪派对人工智能科技有限公司专注于人工智能陪伴类应用的研发。我们正在开发「奶爪派对」App：用户可以领养一只 AI 小狗或小猫，它有自己的性格和小日子，会记得和你聊过的事。

Nanjing Naizhua Paidui Artificial Intelligence Technology Co., Ltd. develops AI companion software. Our app, Milkpaw, lets adult users adopt an AI dog or cat with its own personality and daily life, which remembers the conversations you share.

产品 Product

奶爪派对 App 目前处于开发阶段，尚未上架。App 内的聊天对象由人工智能生成，并会在界面中明确标注；产品仅面向成年用户。

Milkpaw is currently in development and not yet available in app stores. All companion conversations are generated by AI and clearly labeled as such in the app. The service is intended for adults only.
